Neer - Privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026 · Tin Compass Studio

The short version

Neer turns an apartment's water meter readings into monthly bills. To do that it keeps the apartment's people, readings and bills on a server, so every phone in the building sees the same records. We use that information only to run the app for your apartment. We don't sell it, and we don't use it for advertising.

What Neer keeps

  • About people: name, email address, flat number, owner or tenant, role (admin, watchman, resident) and language. An apartment's admin adds these; you can change your language and email preferences yourself.
  • About the apartment: its name and city, flats, rates, tank sizes, tanker deliveries and costs, bore electricity readings and the Manjeera bill.
  • Readings and bills: each flat's monthly meter reading, who took it and when, an optional photo of the meter, and the issued bills.
  • The apartment's Gmail: the address and an app password the admin enters, used only to send sign-in codes and bills. The password stays on the server and is never sent back to any phone.
  • Purchases: Google Play's receipt for Neer Plus or Neer Pro (a purchase token, product, and when it ends), so the plan can be switched on. We never see card or bank details.
  • An activity log of admin actions (rates changed, bills issued, people added), visible to the apartment's admins.

Who can see it

  • Only people in your apartment, and only what their role allows: residents see their own flat's readings and bills; the watchman sees readings but not costs; admins see everything for their apartment.
  • Bills are emailed from the apartment's own Gmail to the people of each flat who chose to get them.
  • Service providers that run the app for us, under their own privacy terms: Supabase (database, sign-in and storage, hosted in Mumbai, India), Cloudflare (a relay the app connects through), Google (Gmail for sending, Google Play for purchases, AdMob for ads).

Ads

On the free plan Neer shows small banner ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar information to show and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policies. Where the law asks for it, the app asks for consent first, and Settings has "Ad privacy choices" to change your answer. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Apartments on Neer Plus, and people with Neer Pro, see no ads.

How long it's kept

  • Readings, bills and the activity log: as long as the apartment uses Neer, because they are the apartment's financial records.
  • Meter photos: in full for two years, then only a small preview.
  • Your account: until you delete it, or an admin removes you from the apartment.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete my account. This removes your sign-in and your place in the apartment straight away. If you are the only person in an apartment, the whole apartment and its photos are deleted too. If you are an apartment's only admin, hand your role to someone first (People → Admins).

Bills already issued stay in the apartment's records, as they are its accounts. Purchase receipts are kept without your name, as Google Play and tax rules require.

Can't use the app? Email tincompass.support@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, and we'll delete the account within 30 days.

Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in is by a one-time code sent to your email; Neer has no passwords of its own. The server checks every change against your role, whatever the app sends.

Children

Neer is for running an apartment's water accounts and is not meant for children under 13.
